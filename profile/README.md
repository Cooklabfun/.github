## What is CookLab?

CookLab is a social-native token launchpad built on Solana.

Creators can launch tokens through Telegram, while communities can discover and trade them through transparent bonding-curve markets. Launches follow consistent protocol rules, fixed token supplies, creator vesting, and verifiable on-chain settlement.

## What we're building

- Social-native token launches
- Telegram-based market interactions
- Transparent bonding-curve trading
- Fixed-supply Solana tokens
- Graduation-gated creator vesting
- Permanent post-graduation liquidity
- Live market discovery and leaderboards
- Verifiable on-chain market activity

## How CookLab works

1. A creator starts a launch through the CookLab Telegram bot.
2. CookLab creates a fixed-supply Solana token.
3. The token enters a transparent bonding-curve market.
4. Community members buy and sell through social interfaces.
5. The market graduates after reaching its protocol target.
6. Liquidity transitions into the permanent CookLab liquidity system.

## Our principles

- **Social first:** Markets begin where communities already communicate.
- **Transparent:** Launch and trading activity is independently verifiable.
- **Consistent:** Every market follows the same protocol rules.
- **Community driven:** Market momentum comes from real participation.
- **Solana native:** Fast settlement and low-cost execution.
